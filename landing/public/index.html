FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an SI seller?

SI stands for Super Intelligence. It’s your AI-powered seller: it chats with clients using your materials and your method, and calls you when a real person is needed.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Everything is set up in plain words: you answer questions about your work, and SmartFlow builds the SI seller for you. You can edit it any time.

Won’t SI make things up?

SI answers from your materials and FAQ. If it doesn’t know something, it doesn’t guess — it passes the question to you, and the client waits for your reply.

How will clients reach my SI seller?

Through your link — from social media, stories or your bio — or via the SmartFlow Marketplace. You can create several links and see which one brings more clients.

How will I know a client wants to talk to me?

You’ll get a notification on your phone or in Telegram, and the client appears in your inbox. You continue the conversation yourself while SI stays quiet.

How do I sign up?

Tap “Start for free” and sign in with your e-mail (you’ll get a 6-digit code) or with Google. If you prefer Telegram, open SmartFlow in Telegram.
