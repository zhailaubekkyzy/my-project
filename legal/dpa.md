# Smartflow Data Processing Addendum

**Version v1.1 · November 27, 2026**

## 1. Parties and scope

This Addendum is part of the Terms of Service between [Smartflow, Inc.] ("Processor") and each business, expert or marketer that uses Smartflow to talk to its own clients ("Customer", the controller). Until [Smartflow, Inc.] is formed, the Processor is Individual Entrepreneur Gulsara (Tomiris) Zhailaubekkyzy, [IE address], Kazakhstan, who may assign this Addendum to [Smartflow, Inc.] on its formation. It applies when Smartflow processes personal data of the Customer's clients on the Customer's behalf. It is accepted together with the Terms.

## 2. Processing details

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Subject matter | Running Smart consultants, funnels, chats, calendar booking and analytics for the Customer |
| Duration | While the Customer's account is active, plus the deletion period in section 8 |
| Nature and purpose | Storing, analyzing and answering client messages with AI; booking meetings; showing funnel statuses and analytics |
| Data subjects | The Customer's clients and prospects; the Customer's team members |
| Personal data | Name, Telegram ID and username, phone, email, messages and voice messages, meeting times, funnel status, usage data |
| Special categories | None intended. The Customer must not collect them through Smart consultants without a legal basis |

## 3. Processor duties

Smartflow will:

- process client data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which are these Terms and the Customer's settings in the Services;
- not use client data for its own purposes, except to create aggregated, de-identified statistics that identify no person or Customer;
- tell the Customer if it believes an instruction breaks data protection law;
- make sure its staff and contractors are bound by confidentiality;
- keep the security measures in section 6;
- help the Customer answer data subject requests and carry out impact assessments, where reasonable;
- give the Customer the information needed to show compliance, including answers to a reasonable written security questionnaire once a year. On-site audits take place only when a supervisory authority requires them, on 30 days' notice and at the Customer's cost.

## 4. Customer duties

The Customer has a legal basis for the client data it collects and gives clients its own privacy notice. It tells clients they are talking to an AI and does not use the Services for spam.

## 5. Subprocessors

The Customer authorizes Smartflow to use the subprocessors listed in section 5 of the Privacy Policy: Supabase, Railway, OpenAI, PostHog, Amplitude, Resend, [SMS provider], Telegram, Google and Whop. Smartflow binds each one to data protection terms no weaker than this Addendum and stays responsible for them. We will announce a new subprocessor at least 14 days in advance. The Customer may object on reasonable grounds and may close its account if we cannot resolve the objection.

## 6. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest; row-level access rules in the database; access only for people who need it; API keys in secret storage; regular backups; logs of admin access.

## 7. International transfers

Client data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Where it is transferred out of the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2 or 3, as applicable) are incorporated into this Addendum by reference, or the recipient is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## 8. Breaches and deletion

Smartflow will notify the Customer without undue delay, and no later than 72 hours, after it learns of a breach affecting client data. The notice will say what happened, what data was affected and what we are doing. Where all details are not yet known, we will send them in stages.

When the Customer's account ends, Smartflow deletes client data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it. The Customer can export its client list before closing the account.

## 9. Order of precedence

If this Addendum and the Terms conflict on data protection, this Addendum wins. If the Standard Contractual Clauses apply, they win over both.
