Smartflow is a platform where marketers build sales funnels, businesses and experts run AI consultants ("Smart consultants") on them, and end clients chat with those consultants.

**Controller.** [Smartflow, Inc.], [registered address]. Until [Smartflow, Inc.] is formed, the Services are operated by Individual Entrepreneur Gulsara (Tomiris) Zhailaubekkyzy, [IE address], Kazakhstan, BIN [number]. On formation, the operator will transfer this Policy and your data to [Smartflow, Inc.], and we will notify you.

**Two roles.** For data about marketers, businesses and their own use of the Services, Smartflow is the controller. If you are a client of a business on Smartflow, that business's own privacy policy also applies.

## 4. AI and Google user data

**AI disclosure.** Smart consultants and the AI buddy are artificial intelligence, not people. Each chat with a Smart consultant shows clearly that the client is talking to an AI. A client can ask for a human, and the conversation is then marked "waiting for a human" for the business.

**How AI processing works.** To generate replies, funnels, scripts, voice transcripts and media, we send the needed text and audio to OpenAI through its API. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train OpenAI's models. OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse, then deletes it. We send only the data a task needs. We do not use your conversations to train our own models.

**AI can make mistakes.** Businesses are responsible for checking what their Smart consultants promise to clients.

**Google user data.** If you sign in with Google or connect Google Calendar, we request only:

- `openid`, `email`, `profile`: your name, email and photo, to create and sign in to your account;
- Google Calendar access: to show your free times, and to create, update and delete meetings you or your Smart consultant book.

We use Google data only to provide these features to you. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. People can read it only with your consent, for security reasons, or when the law requires.

Smartflow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account permissions or in Smartflow settings. We then delete the Google data we stored within 30 days.

## 6. Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. After you delete your account, we delete or anonymize it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it longer. Backups are overwritten within [90] days. We keep a business's client conversations while the business's account is active, unless the business deletes them earlier.

## 7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Access is limited to people who need it, API keys are kept in secret storage, and databases use row-level access rules. No system is fully secure, so we will tell you and the authorities about a data breach where the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA or the laws of Kazakhstan), you can:

- access your data and get a copy;
- correct it;
- delete it ("Delete account" in settings, or by email);
- restrict or object to processing, including analytics;
- withdraw consent at any time;
- complain to your local data protection authority.

Write to Tomiris@techsmartflow.com. If you are a client of a business on Smartflow, we will pass your request to that business or help it answer. We do not sell or "share" personal data for cross-context advertising, as these terms are defined in the CCPA.

## 9. Children

The Services are for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If we learn we have, we delete it. Parents can contact us at the address above.

## 10. Cookies and analytics

Our website and app use strictly necessary cookies and local storage to keep you signed in. With your consent, we also use PostHog and Amplitude to understand how the Services are used. Visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland see a cookie banner, and analytics load only after they agree. You can change your choice at any time in the cookie settings at the bottom of the site. We do not send message content to analytics tools.
